GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is about getting your brand mentioned and cited inside AI answers — Google's AI overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity. It involves structuring your content so AI systems can read and quote it: clear direct answers, proper schema markup, named expertise and specific facts. It works alongside classic SEO, not instead of it.

Yes — but the goal has shifted. AI answers still need sources, and they cite websites that are clear, credible and well-structured.
