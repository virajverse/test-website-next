The Definition

So What Actually Is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization is the work of shaping your website, content, and online authority so AI-powered search systems can confidently understand, trust, and recommend your business inside their generated answers.

Unlike traditional SEO, GEO isn't chasing a ranking position. It's aiming at getting referenced by AI assistants, showing up in AI summaries, being recommended in conversational search, and recognised as an authority in your space — preparing your business for how people are searching right now, and how they'll be searching a year from now.

Why GEO Matters

AI Platforms Aren't Counting Keywords

They're weighing whether your content genuinely answers the question someone asked. Businesses that put real effort into these areas improve their odds of showing up across multiple AI platforms — not just one.

  • Does your content genuinely answer the question
  • Demonstrated expertise and accuracy
  • Context and topical authority
  • How trustworthy your site feels
  • Whether your information is structured
  • The actual user experience
  • Brand consistency across the web
  • How fresh your content is

What Changes

How GEO Is Different From Traditional SEO

Traditional SEO and GEO aren't competing — they work together, aimed at different goals.

Traditional SEO

"Can Google rank this page?"

  • Optimises keywords and metadata
  • Builds backlinks
  • Improves technical performance
  • Aims at a ranking position

Generative Engine Optimization

"Would an AI system trust this page enough to include it in an answer?"

  • Helps AI understand how topics relate
  • Evaluates and builds your authority
  • Signals a trustworthy source
  • Aims at a confident recommendation
