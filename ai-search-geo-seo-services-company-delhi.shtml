The Definitions

What Exactly Is AI SEO — And GEO?

AI SEO

The practice of shaping your website so AI systems can understand, trust and reference what you offer — not just crawl and index it.

Traditional SEO is a rankings game. AI SEO asks a different question: can a language model read this page and come away confident enough to recommend it? The goal is simple — become one of the sources an AI system reaches for when it builds an answer in your space.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

The next layer up. Where SEO is built around clicks and rankings, GEO is built around getting cited, quoted, summarized or recommended inside an AI-generated response. AI platforms weigh far more than keyword density:

  • Depth and quality of your content
  • Real topical authority, not isolated posts
  • Context that answers the actual question
  • How your topics connect to related ones
  • Overall site trustworthiness
  • Structured, machine-readable information
  • Brand reputation across the web
  • Factual accuracy and content freshness
