Technical SEO Services in Delhi

The Foundation Your Website Actually Needs

A beautiful website doesn't count for much if search engines can't find their way around it, or if visitors give up halfway through a slow-loading homepage. That's the part nobody likes talking about — technical SEO.

Get a Free Technical SEO Audit Talk to Our SEO Experts

It's not glamorous, and it's not the kind of work that shows up in a portfolio screenshot. But without it, everything else you do for rankings is standing on sand.

We spend a lot of our time at DigifyNext untangling exactly this stuff: pages that refuse to get indexed, load times that crawl, broken links nobody noticed, duplicate content fighting itself for rankings, Core Web Vitals scores that won't move no matter what you try. Our job isn't to slap a patch on the symptom — it's to find out why it's happening in the first place, and fix that.

Why It Matters

Why Technical SEO Matters More Than Ever

Here's a pattern we run into constantly: a business publishes blog after blog, invests in link building for months, and rankings barely budge. Nine times out of ten, the content isn't actually the problem. The website is.

Google's whole goal is delivering the best possible experience to someone typing in a search query — and it can't do that when a site loads slowly, throws crawl errors, has a structure that makes no sense, or falls apart on a phone screen. When any of that happens, pages just don't get crawled and indexed properly, regardless of how well-written they are.

That's what technical SEO fixes. Rather than guessing why rankings have plateaued, it tells you, specifically, what's actually wrong.

The Gap

Common Technical SEO Problems We Find

Most site owners have no idea how many small technical issues are quietly working against them until someone actually goes looking. We see the same handful over and over.

Slow page speed Weak Core Web Vitals Broken internal or external links Duplicate content Missing canonical tags Crawl errors Indexing gaps Misconfigured robots.txt Sitemap errors Redirect chains Stray 404s JavaScript rendering quirks Mobile usability problems Thin pages that shouldn't be indexed Site architecture that makes no sense

On their own, none of these will sink a website. Stack five or six together, though, and you've got a site that search engines genuinely struggle to crawl, understand, and rank.

The Definition

So What Actually Is Technical SEO?

Technical SEO is the work of optimizing your website's underlying infrastructure so search engines can crawl, index, and actually understand your pages — without a fight.

Traditional SEO leans on keywords and content. Technical SEO operates one layer down: performance, user experience, site architecture, page speed, security, structured data, crawlability. It's less visible, and it's also the part that determines whether any of the rest of your SEO work has a real chance.

Your Content

Technical SEO

The foundation. Skip it and everything above wobbles.

Picture your website as a building. Skip the foundation, and everything you build on top starts to wobble sooner or later.

The Real Issue

Why Your Site Might Not Be Ranking

It's tempting to assume the fix is just "more content." More often, the real issue is buried somewhere you're not looking.

  • Pages Google literally can't crawl
  • Important pages accidentally excluded from the index
  • Several duplicate versions of the same page competing against each other
  • Images dragging load times down
  • A URL structure that confuses more than it clarifies
  • Broken navigation
  • Internal links that don't signal what actually matters
  • Missing structured data
  • A mobile experience that falls short
  • Server performance quietly working against you

Until that gets sorted out, even genuinely good content struggles to reach the audience it deserves.

Website Crawl Optimization

Search engines only allocate so much crawl budget to any given site. We make sure yours gets spent on the pages that matter, not wasted on ones that don't.

03

Core Web Vitals Optimization

Speed isn't a nice-to-have anymore. We work on Largest Contentful Paint, Interaction to Next Paint, and Cumulative Layout Shift until performance holds up across devices — phone, tablet, whatever your visitors are actually using.

04

Page Speed Optimization

Usually this means image optimization, cleaning up CSS and JavaScript, better browser caching, faster server response times, lazy loading, compression, and CDN integration where it makes sense.

05

Website Architecture Optimization

A clean, logical structure helps people and search engines find their way around your site. We organize pages so authority flows where it should, instead of getting stuck.

06

XML Sitemap Optimization

We build and maintain sitemaps so search engines find your important pages quickly, instead of stumbling onto them eventually — or not at all.

07

Robots.txt Optimization

It's shockingly easy for a misconfigured robots.txt file to block pages you actually want indexed. We go through it line by line and fix what's blocking valuable content.

08

Canonical Tag Optimization

Duplicate pages confuse search engines about which version to rank. Canonical tags settle that question before it becomes a problem.

09

Schema Markup Implementation

Structured data tells search engines what your business actually does. Depending on your site, that might mean Organization, Local Business, Service, FAQ, Article, Breadcrumb, Product, Review, or WebPage schema.

10

HTTPS & Security Review

Security is a ranking factor now, not an afterthought. We check your SSL setup and flag anything undermining trust or performance.

11

Mobile SEO Optimization

Most searches happen on a phone these days. We make sure responsiveness, usability, navigation, and load times all hold up regardless of screen size.

12

Internal Linking Strategy

Good internal linking does two jobs at once — it helps visitors find what they need, and it tells search engines how your pages relate to each other.

13

Redirect & Error Management

404 errors, bad 301s, redirect chains, redirect loops, soft 404s — we clean all of it up so you're not quietly losing SEO value to broken plumbing.
