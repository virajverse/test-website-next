The Definition

So What Actually Is Local SEO?

Local SEO is the work of improving how visible your business is in location-based search — Maps, the Local Pack, "near me" searches, all of it.

Where traditional SEO chases broader rankings, Local SEO zeroes in on one specific city, locality, or service area. It pulls together website optimization, Google Business Profile management, citations, reviews, location pages, and local content — all aimed at one outcome: showing up where it actually matters for your business.

It's not really about traffic. It's about getting in front of the people who are ready to call, visit, or buy.
